Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Everything students need to know about registering for classes.

Go to the Course Catalog tab, browse or filter through the subjects, and click the blue "Enroll Now" button. The portal will immediately reserve your seat and add the lecture to your weekly class schedule.

Courses show live color-coded seat indicators (Green: available, Orange: seats going, Red 🔥: filling fast with 15% or less left). When a course reaches 0 seats left, you can click "Join Waitlist". When a seat opens up (e.g. another student drops), the first student in the waitlist queue is immediately notified with a blue banner to claim the seat!

Yes! Our curriculum encourages interdisciplinary learning. Engineering students can pick Arts electives (like Digital Journalism or Visual Arts), and Arts students can take Programming or CAD Modeling.

We recommend between 12 and 18 credits per semester (usually 4 to 5 courses). The maximum allowed cap per semester is 22 credits.

Go to the Class Timetable tab and click the blue "Add to Google Calendar (.ics)" button. It downloads a standard .ics calendar file which automatically syncs all recurring class schedules directly into your phone or laptop calendar.

Navigate to My Enrolled Courses or directly on the course card in the catalog and click the red "Drop" button. A friendly confirmation popup will ask you to confirm before freeing up the seat for waitlisted students.
